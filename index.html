PABLO PARAISO

Named after the Spanish phrase for "Paul's Paradise," Pablo Paraiso is a serene lakeside retreat where summer celebrations come to life. Our pool house sits on the gentle shores of Laguna de Bay, offering stunning water views, full amenities, and a carefully curated atmosphere for every occasion.

  • Prime LocationJust 30 minutes from Manila, nestled along the scenic shores of Laguna de Bay.
  • Pool & VillaSwimming pool (3ft–5ft depth) with a one-room villa featuring a private toilet and bathroom.
  • All AmenitiesTwo shower rooms, dining tables, grill, videoke, and lush garden grounds.
  • Flexible Pricing6-hour and 10-hour packages starting at ₱4,000, plus custom event options.

Everything You Need

Thoughtfully designed amenities for unforgettable experiences.

Pool

Swimming pool with depths ranging from 3ft to 5ft — perfect for both casual lounging and deep-end fun.

Villa

One-room villa with a private toilet and bathroom, ideal for changing, rest, or overnight accommodation.

Shower Rooms

Two clean shower rooms with hot and cold water, complete with towels and basic toiletries.

Tables

Dining and event tables in various configurations, perfect for meals, meetings, or games.

Grill

State-of-the-art charcoal and gas grills for your barbecue needs, with premium cuts available.

Videoke

Complete videoke setup with microphone, speaker system, and a wide selection of songs.

Garden

Lush garden grounds surrounding the pool house, perfect for outdoor ceremonies or quiet relaxation.

Mini-Golf

A fun 4-hole mini-golf course set amidst the gardens — perfect for friendly competitions and casual play.
